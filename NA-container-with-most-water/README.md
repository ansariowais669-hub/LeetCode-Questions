## 🧩 Problem — Container With Most Water

### Problem Statement

You are given an integer array `height` of length `n`.

There are `n` vertical lines such that the two endpoints of the `i`-th line are at `(i, 0)` and `(i, height[i])`.

Choose two lines that, together with the x-axis, form a container that can hold water.

Return the **maximum amount of water** that the container can store.

The container cannot be tilted.
